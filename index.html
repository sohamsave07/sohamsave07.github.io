About Me

I started out as an aspiring Data Scientist, chasing the classic dream of building models that could predict, classify, and explain the world a little better.
That path led me to Dell Technologies, where I grew into an MLOps Engineer — automating ETL pipelines with Airflow, fine-tuning embedding models with MLflow, and shipping everything through CI/CD into Docker and Kubernetes so models didn't just work in a notebook, they worked in production.
From there the work kept pulling me forward: from pipelines that move data to agents that take action. Today I design and build AI agents end-to-end — orchestrating multi-agent systems as an MCP client connected to MCP servers, building RAG-powered chatbots, and shipping agents on platforms like Glean that draft outreach emails, generate meeting agendas, and turn business processes into structured, actionable content.
Same curiosity that started with "can I predict this?" now shows up as "can I build something that does this on its own?"
